    Material & finish

    The beauty of a clean finish.

    A mirror polish turns a simple disc into something that catches the light — and leaves the surface clear for the words you choose.

    Polished surfaces show fingerprints and fine marks with everyday wear. A soft cloth is all it needs; avoid abrasive cleaners, which can dull the finish.
