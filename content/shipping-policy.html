SlateStream ships stainless steel linear drainage Australia-wide. Because a linear drain is a long item, it is handled as oversized freight rather than as a parcel, and that affects how it is packed, who carries it and where it can be delivered. Please read this policy before you order.

Where we ship

We ship to addresses across Australia, including regional and remote areas.

What shipping costs

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and the length of the drain you have ordered. Enter your address at checkout to see the exact cost before you pay. We do not publish flat rates, because freight for a 1500mm item to regional Queensland is not the same as freight for a 600mm item across a capital city.

Dispatch and lead times

  • Standard sizes — ships within 2 business days.
  • Custom drains — 10 to 15 business days, as each one is made to your specified length and outlet configuration.

Dispatch time is the time before your order leaves us. Transit time is additional and depends on your location and the carrier. We do not guarantee a delivery date. If you are working to a build program and need the drain on site by a particular day, contact us before you order and we will tell you honestly whether it is achievable.

Oversized freight — please read

Linear drains up to 1500mm cannot be sent through standard parcel networks. This means:

  • Delivery must go to a street address. We cannot deliver to PO boxes, parcel lockers or collection points.
  • Someone needs to be available to receive and sign for the delivery. Most carriers will not leave an oversized item unattended.
  • If your site has restricted access, a delivery window, or requires a specific entrance, tell us when you order rather than after dispatch. Redirecting freight in transit is expensive and slow.

Pickup

Pickup is not available. All orders are shipped.

Tracking

You will receive tracking details by email once your order is dispatched. If you have not received them within one business day of your dispatch confirmation, contact us.

Damage in transit

Inspect your drain on arrival, before you sign for it if you can. If the packaging or the drain is visibly damaged, note it with the driver and photograph it. Then contact us within 48 hours of delivery with the photographs and your order number so we can resolve it with the carrier. Claims are considerably harder to make once the item has been installed or the packaging discarded.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses

Please check your delivery address carefully at checkout. If an order is returned to us or needs redirecting because the address was incorrect or incomplete, the cost of re-delivery is payable by you.

Orders that have not arrived

If tracking shows your order as delivered but you do not have it, or if tracking has not updated for several business days, contact us with your order number and we will follow it up with the carrier.

International orders

Our pricing and lead times are set for Australian delivery. If you are ordering from outside Australia, contact us before placing the order so we can confirm freight, transit time and any duties or taxes that would apply at your end. Import duties and taxes are your responsibility.
